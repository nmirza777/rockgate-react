Privacy policy.

This notice explains how information provided through this website or by email is handled.

Rockgate Capital respects your privacy and aims to handle personal information fairly, securely, and transparently. This website privacy notice applies to general website and email enquiries. If your enquiry progresses further, you may receive a more detailed privacy notice before additional personal or financial information is collected.

Information we may collect

We may collect your name, contact details, the contents of your enquiry, and any information you choose to provide by email. Basic technical and security information may also be recorded by the website hosting provider.

How we use your information

We use information to respond to enquiries, communicate with you, maintain appropriate business records, protect the website, and meet legal or regulatory obligations where they apply.

Our lawful basis

Depending on the circumstances, we process information because it is necessary to take steps at your request, because we have a legitimate interest in responding to and managing enquiries, or because we must comply with a legal obligation.

Sharing information

We may share information with trusted technology or professional service providers, or with legal and regulatory bodies where required. We do not sell personal information. Further sharing connected with a mortgage or finance application would be explained before that process begins.

Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, and for any applicable legal or regulatory requirements. Appropriate technical and organisational measures are used to protect it.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, transfer, or object to the use of your information. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office if you remain concerned about how your information has been handled.

Cookies and website data

This version of the website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies. Essential technical logs may be created by the hosting provider. This notice and any consent controls should be updated before optional analytics, advertising pixels, or similar technologies are introduced.

Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email info@rockgatecapital.co.uk. Please do not send sensitive financial documents until a secure method has been agreed.
